Rollover policies

Reset to zero — classic envelope. Leftover or overspend disappears on the 1st.

Carry deficit — if you go over, next month's envelope is reduced by the overspend. Used for Sins.

Sweep to piggy — on the 1st, leftover moves into a savings goal as a real bank transfer. Used for Travel and Lifestyle.
